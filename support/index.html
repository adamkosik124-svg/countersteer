Support

Need help with Project Countersteer, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to:

adam.kosik.dev@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, if you can, a short description or screenshot of what happened. I usually reply within a few days.

Beta testers

If you are testing through TestFlight, the easiest way to send feedback is from the TestFlight app (or take a screenshot in the game and tap Share Beta Feedback). Crash reports are sent through TestFlight automatically if you allow it.

Common questions

How do I reset my progress? Deleting the app removes all saved progress, lap times and settings from your iPhone.

How do I hide my name from the leaderboards? Game Center is optional. You can sign out of Game Center or change your nickname and privacy settings in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center.
