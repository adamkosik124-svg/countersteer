Privacy Policy

Project Countersteer (iPhone app) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Project Countersteer is a racing game made by one independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. In short: the game shows no ads, does not track you, and never sells data. Only if you use the optional ghost battles or, if you say yes, the drive-log sharing described below, a small amount of game data is sent to the game's own server in Germany.

1. Who is responsible

The developer and data controller is Adam Kosík, Czech Republic. Contact for anything about privacy: adam.kosik.dev@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your iPhone

To work as a game, Project Countersteer saves some information locally on your device:

  • Game progress (for example finished tutorial steps, chosen cars and car setups).
  • Your settings (controls, driving assists, sound, vibration, screen layout).
  • Your lap times, best laps and replay/ghost data of your own laps.
  • In test (beta) versions only: small technical logs such as frame rate and control input records, used to find bugs and performance problems.

This information stays on your iPhone, except for the two optional features in sections 3a and 3b. It may be included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) according to your Apple settings.

3. Game Center (optional, provided by Apple)

The game can use Apple's Game Center for sign-in and leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best lap time on each circuit is submitted to the Game Center leaderboard, together with your Game Center player identity (nickname and avatar), so you and other players can compare times.

Game Center is run by Apple, not by me. Apple processes this information under the Apple Privacy Policy. I can see leaderboard entries the same way other players do, but I receive no email, real name or contact details from Game Center. Game Center is optional: you can play the whole game without it, sign out at any time, and control your profile and visibility in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center.

3a. Ghost battles (optional, the game's own server)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best time-trial lap on each circuit to the game's own server so other players can race against it as a "ghost" car. What is sent: your Game Center player ID (used only to keep one best lap per player and never shown to other players), your Game Center display name, the car class and car, the lap time, and a recording of the lap (the car's position, speed and steering along the lap). Other players see your display name, car and lap time on the ghost board. The server is a rented server run by me at Hetzner in Germany (EU). The data is not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising. It is kept until you ask me to delete it (write to the address below with your Game Center name and I will remove all your laps), or until the ghost feature is shut down. Without Game Center, nothing is sent.

3b. Drive logs (optional, asked once, can be turned off)

The first time you open the game it asks whether you want to share anonymous drive logs to help me find bugs and improve how the car drives. If you say yes, after each race or time trial the game sends a short log of that drive to the game's own server in Germany: a summary taken about 20 times a second (speed, steering, throttle and brake inputs, position on the circuit, lap times), your driving-assist settings, the circuit and car, the app version, and the device model with frame-rate statistics. The log carries only a random number generated by the game on your iPhone; it contains no name, no email, no Game Center identity, and no location. I cannot tell who you are from it. If you are offline, the log is sent the next time you are online. Logs are deleted automatically from the server after 90 days. You can say no at the first question, or turn sharing off at any time in Settings; nothing is sent while it is off. If you want logs from your phone deleted sooner, write to me with the log number shown in Settings.

4. Beta testing through TestFlight (provided by Apple)

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares certain information with me as described in Apple's TestFlight terms: for example your name and email address as entered for the invitation, the app version and device model, crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you choose to send. I use this information only to run the beta test, fix problems and improve the game, and I do not share it with anyone. Apple processes it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Support emails

If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report, and I delete it when it is no longer needed for that.

6. What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no analytics or tracking tools from third parties.
  • No accounts of my own (the ghost and drive-log features use your Game Center ID or a random number only as described above); no location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No in-app purchases at this time.
  • Your data is never sold, rented or traded.

7. Why the information is used (legal basis)

Locally stored data, Game Center leaderboards and ghost battles are used only to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Drive logs are sent only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Beta test data, crash reports and support messages are used to fix bugs and improve the game (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Using Game Center is your choice and you can stop at any time.

8. Sharing and third parties

The ghost and drive-log data is stored on a server I rent from Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany (EU), which provides the hardware and has no use of the data. The only other third party involved is Apple, as the provider of the App Store, TestFlight and Game Center. Apple is bound by its own privacy policy and offers protection of your data equal to or greater than this policy. I do not share your information with any other company or person, except where the law requires it.

9. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Data on your iPhone is kept until you delete it. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device (copies inside your own backups follow your backup settings).
  • Game Center data is kept by Apple. You can sign out, change your nickname and visibility, or ask Apple to delete your data through Apple's Data and Privacy page at privacy.apple.com. You can also ask me to remove a leaderboard entry.
  • TestFlight data (crash reports, feedback) is kept only while needed for testing and is deleted when no longer needed, at the latest when the beta ends. You can stop testing at any time in the TestFlight app; you can ask me to delete your tester information sooner.
  • Ghost laps on the game's server are kept until you ask me to delete them.
  • Drive logs on the game's server are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Support emails are deleted when the matter is resolved.

10. Your rights (EU / GDPR and similar laws)

You have the right to ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing. Optional features such as Game Center are your choice; you can stop using them at any time, for example by signing out of Game Center. To use any of these rights, write to adam.kosik.dev@gmail.com; I will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz) or the authority in your own country.

11. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Game Center features for children are controlled by Apple's Screen Time and Family Sharing settings. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent me personal information (for example by email), contact me and I will delete it.

12. Security

Game data stays on your device, protected by iOS, apart from the optional data in sections 3a and 3b. The game's server uses encrypted connections (https), a firewall, key-only administrator login and automatic security updates; its data is readable only by me. Information I receive from Apple or by email is kept in password-protected accounts and is accessed only by me.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example new online features or purchases), this page will be updated before the change is released, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

14. Contact

Adam Kosík, Czech Republic · adam.kosik.dev@gmail.com
